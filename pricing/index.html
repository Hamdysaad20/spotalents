PRICING FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about annual subscriptions, talent engagement fees, AI credit allowances, and commercial terms.

001
What does my annual subscription include?

Your subscription includes access to SpotTalents' platform, talent intelligence features, plan-specific AI capabilities, and a monthly AI credit allowance.
002
Is the talent engagement fee included?

No. The subscription and talent engagement fee are separate. An individually agreed percentage applies to a talent's monthly compensation when an engagement begins.
003
When do I start paying the engagement fee?

Once you select a talent and the working relationship officially begins, according to the agreed engagement terms.
004
Is the engagement fee a one-time payment?

No. It is a recurring monthly fee calculated as an agreed percentage of the talent's monthly compensation while the engagement is active.
005
Do I pay if I don't hire anyone?

No talent engagement fee applies if you do not start an engagement. Your annual subscription remains active and provides access to your subscribed platform features.
006
Can engagement percentages differ between talents?

Yes. Engagement percentages are individually agreed based on the talent, compensation, engagement structure, and commercial terms.
007
Can I purchase additional AI credits?

Yes. Every subscription includes a monthly allowance, and additional credits can be purchased separately with your approval.
008
Do unused AI credits roll over?

Monthly subscription allowances refresh each month and do not roll over. Purchased credit packages follow their stated validity and usage terms.
009
Can I upgrade to Enterprise?

Yes. Enterprise plans offer customized access, intelligence requirements, usage allowances, and commercial agreements.
010
How are talent compensation and platform fees calculated?

Talent compensation is agreed separately. The SpotTalents engagement fee is calculated using the agreed percentage and compensation basis specified in the engagement agreement.
