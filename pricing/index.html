PRICING FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about individual talent pricing, team composition, and flexible workforce arrangements.

001
How is individual talent priced?

Each engagement is priced according to the engineer's experience, specialization, seniority, availability, and engagement terms.
002
Can I engage just one engineer?

Yes. You can engage an individual specialist for your project without purchasing a predefined or locked team package.
003
Can I build a team with different seniority levels?

Yes. You can combine junior, mid-level, senior, lead, and principal engineers according to your project requirements.
004
Does Spotalents recommend the team composition?

Yes. Spotalents Intelligence recommends individuals and pod combinations based on technical synergy and role compatibility.
005
Can I change my workforce requirements?

Yes. Engagements can be scaled up, down, or adjusted according to project availability, sprint pace, and delivery terms.
006
Are there additional management or placement fees?

No. All applicable fees and talent engagement rates are communicated with complete transparency before work begins.
