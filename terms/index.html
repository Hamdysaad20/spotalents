2. Platform Intelligence Services

Spotalents provides an AI-powered talent intelligence platform that analyzes multi-signal candidate delivery data—including git commit velocity, pull request reviews, test coverage benchmarks, and team compatibility indicators—to match companies with elite individual engineers and coordinated engineering pods.

3. Platform Subscription Pathways & AI Credits

Platform intelligence access is organized into subscription tiers:

  • Discover: $99/month (or $82.50/month billed annually at $990/year), including 1,000 monthly AI matching credits.
  • Intelligence: $299/month (or $249/month billed annually at $2,990/year), including 5,000 monthly AI matching credits and complete pod assembly.
  • Enterprise: Tailored agreements with custom AI credit pools, dedicated SLA agreements, and regional compliance auditing.

Monthly credit allowances refresh at the start of each billing cycle and do not roll over. Annual subscriptions are billed upfront with a 20% discount as indicated in our commercial schedule.

4. Zero-Risk Talent Placement & Engagement Fee (10%)

When a client elects to engage and deploy talent recommended through Spotalents:

  • Transparent 10% Rate: Spotalents charges a simple, transparent 10% matching fee on top of developer base compensation, billed only for active working time.
  • Zero Upfront Retainers: Browsing candidate match reports and conducting initial pod calibrations requires zero upfront hiring deposits.
  • 14-Day Replacement Guarantee: If an engineer or pod does not integrate smoothly within the first 14 business days of active deployment, Spotalents replaces the talent with zero additional matching fee or refunds the initial fee prorated.
