Privacy Policy

1. Overview & Data Controller

Spotalents (“we,” “our,” or “us”), operated by Dragons Bootcamp LLC, is committed to safeguarding the privacy and confidentiality of our prospective clients, enterprise partners, and candidate engineers. This Privacy Policy details our operational data handling protocols across spotalents.com, our talent intelligence engines, and our serverless application programming interfaces.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information provided directly by prospective clients when scheduling consultations, calibrating engineering pods, or interacting with our intelligence platforms:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Full name, authorized corporate email address, and professional title.
  • Organization Data: Company or organization name, operational domain, and scale.
  • Technical Requirements: Target technology stacks, delivery pace, sprint goals, and hiring specifications submitted via consultation forms.
  • Commercial Calibration: Selected platform subscription tier (Discover, Intelligence, Enterprise) and budget/compensation parameters.
  • Technical Telemetry: Client IP address, browser user-agent header, submission timestamp, and referral origin utilized exclusively for rate-limiting, DDoS mitigation, and anti-bot verification.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process your corporate and technical information strictly for legitimate commercial and engineering purposes:

  • To calibrate, benchmark, and deploy matched engineering pods aligned with your technology stack and velocity.
  • To coordinate and schedule executive consultations and calibration reviews.
  • To fulfill contractual obligations and provide customer support.
  • To defend our platform against automated scrapers, brute-force spam, and volumetric denial-of-service threats.

4. Proprietary Code & Mutual Non-Disclosure

Spotalents operates under strict confidentiality standards. We never sell, rent, monetize, or publicly disclose client technology architectures, sprint backlogs, or private codebases. All intellectual property, technical specifications, and proprietary code produced by engineers deployed through Spotalents remain 100% the exclusive property of the client.

5. Data Hosting & Sub-Processors

We engage industry-standard, SOC 2 Type II certified cloud infrastructure providers under strict Data Processing Agreements:

  • Supabase Inc.: Managed PostgreSQL database hosting with enforced Row Level Security (RLS) and AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Vercel Inc.: Secure serverless edge compute with automated TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.
  • Resend / Google Workspace: Secure transactional notification dispatch for incoming demo schedules.

6. Security Architecture & Data Retention

We employ defense-in-depth security measures to protect submitted records: database access tokens are restricted strictly to private serverless runtimes (never exposed to client-side code), public database read permissions are revoked via database policies, and network traffic is forced over HTTPS with strict transport security. Inbound consultation records are retained only for the duration of the commercial relationship or until deletion is formally requested.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Regardless of jurisdiction, all corporate and individual contacts retain the right to:

  • Request verification of whether personal or corporate information is held.
  • Receive a copy of your stored records in a portable machine-readable format.
  • Request immediate rectification or permanent erasure of your contact details.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.

To exercise these rights, email our data privacy team directly at privacy@spotalents.com or contact@spotalents.com.
