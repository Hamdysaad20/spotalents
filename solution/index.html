PRICING FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about annual subscriptions, talent engagement fees, AI credit allowances, and commercial terms.

001
What does the platform subscription include?

Your subscription provides continuous access to SpotTalents' talent intelligence engine, verified Git commit audits, AST code depth analysis, team compatibility models, and monthly AI credit allowances.
002
When do I start paying the talent engagement fee?

Only after you select an engineer and active work officially begins. Searching, viewing AST code telemetry, generating AI matches, and interviewing candidates are 100% free with zero obligation.
003
Is the engagement fee a one-time recruitment fee?

No. Traditional headhunters charge $15,000–$30,000 upfront regardless of outcome. SpotTalents charges an agreed monthly percentage (e.g. 10%) on active talent compensation. If the engagement ends, billing stops immediately.
004
What is the 14-day calibration guarantee?

Every placed engineer starts with a 14-day risk-free calibration trial on your real codebase. If technical delivery or team chemistry isn't an exact fit, replace the candidate immediately or cancel with zero penalty.
005
How do monthly AI credits work and can I top up?

Every subscription plan includes a monthly AI credit allowance that refreshes each month. If your sourcing volume surges, you can purchase prepaid top-up credits anytime with transparent pricing and zero surprise billing.
006
Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Yes. You can begin on flexible monthly billing and switch to an annual plan at any time right from your account settings to lock in the 20% discount (2 months free).
