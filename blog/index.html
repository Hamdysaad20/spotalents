FLAGSHIP TURNING POINT OCTOBER 2026 · ASWAN PLUG AND PLAY AFRICA

“We Were Growing, But Without a System”: Inside the Turning Point with Plug and Play Africa

«كنا بنكبر… بس بدون نظام. وده كان أكبر تحدي واجهناه. رحلتنا مع Plug and Play Africa في Aswan Bootcamp كانت نقطة التحول. بدعم منهم و ITIDA و Creativa Innovation Hubs، قدرنا نعيد بناء الشركة بشكل عملي: إطلاق Nimo Platform كنظام موحد للتوظيف، تنظيم العقود والعمليات، والوصول لـ 3000+ Talent وتوقيع العقد رقم 237 وبدء شغل فعلي مع 9 شركات. ببساطة، بنبني نظام بيساعد الشركات توظف وتدير فرقها بدون تعقيد.»

“We were growing, but without a system—and that was our single greatest challenge. Our journey with Plug and Play Africa at the Aswan Bootcamp was the defining turning point. Supported by ITIDA and Creativa Hubs, we rebuilt our operational infrastructure from the ground up: launching Nimo Platform as a unified hiring OS, standardizing contracts, growing our verified talent pool to 3,000+, executing contract #237, and deploying engineering pods across 9 enterprise clients.”

Keynote Panel on Tech Outsourcing: Deliberating regional engineering expansion alongside Dr. Adel Danish (Founder, Silicon Waha & Xceed) and Karima El Hakim (Partner, Plug and Play).
Government & Academic Leadership: In presence of ITIDA CEO Eng. Ahmed El-Zaher, Aswan Governor Eng. Amr Lashin, Aswan Univ President Dr. Louay Saad El-Din Nasrat, and 61 regional tech companies.

Brand Heritage: From Dragons Bootcamp LLC to Nimo Platform and SpotTalents

Why do historical news releases cite Dragons Bootcamp? Dragons Bootcamp LLC is our officially registered parent venture, founded by Hatem Ali and Hamdy Saad to unlock software engineering talent across Egyptian governorates. In our foundational phase, we trained over 5,000 engineers and won the EEA 2025 Youth Award. As client demand accelerated for turnkey engineering pods, we engineered Nimo Platform to automate candidate vetting, AST complexity scoring, and operational contracts. SpotTalents represents the international AI Talent Intelligence Engine that powers our global matchmaking. Every accolade, ministerial partnership (ITIDA, Creativa), and press feature under Dragons Bootcamp LLC is the proven foundation of SpotTalents today.
