Dragons Bootcamp Wins People of Tomorrow Youth Award at EEA 2025

Founders Hatem Ali and Hamdy Saad honored before Egypt's business elite at the Grand Egyptian Museum for unlocking regional tech talent.

SpotTalents Editorial

Company Chronicle

At a ceremony held at the Grand Egyptian Museum, Dragons Bootcamp LLC was honored with the People of Tomorrow Youth Award at the 5th Edition of Egypt’s Entrepreneur Awards (EEA).

Co-founders Hatem Ali and Hamdy Saad were selected by an independent jury of distinguished business leaders including Ayman Abbas, Onsi Sawiris, and Karima El Hakim. The award recognized their groundbreaking work in training and deploying regional software engineers without relying on traditional recruitment agency models.

The recognition served as national validation of Dragons’ methodology, setting the stage for its evolution into Nimo Platform and SpotTalents.
