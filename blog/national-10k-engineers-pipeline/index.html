Hatem Ali: Targeting 10,000 Engineers for Regional Tech Delivery

An executive roadmap to train 10,000 regional engineers in cloud-native and AI architectures, bridging provincial university talent to global employers.

Hatem Ali

Co-Founder & CEO, SpotTalents

In an exclusive feature published by telecommunications trade journal Follow ICT News, CEO Hatem Ali laid out the organization’s vision to upskill 10,000 developers across regional governorates outside Cairo.

Historically, technical hiring in Egypt has concentrated heavily in Cairo and Giza, creating an artificial talent shortage for expanding tech companies while leaving thousands of talented engineers in Fayoum, Mansoura, Assiut, and Aswan underutilized.

“The geographic origin of a developer should never dictate their career trajectory. By introducing verifiable commit telemetry and hands-on war rooms, we provide regional engineers with an irrefutable proof of competence that commands international respect.”

The 10,000-engineer target will be achieved through partnerships with regional universities—including Mansoura National University, Benha University, and Egyptian E-Learning University (EELU)—feeding into SpotTalents’ international matching engine.
