Dragons Unveils Regional Expansion Blueprints Supported by Plug and Play

How Silicon Valley accelerator Plug and Play Africa catalyzed operational systems and opened cross-border enterprise pipelines.

As covered by entrepreneurship media platform Entarabi, Dragons has detailed its regional scaling blueprint supported by Plug and Play Africa.

The publication notes how Plug and Play’s mentorship, corporate network, and investor syndicates catalyzed the transition from a local academy into an automated workforce matching infrastructure.

Through standardized contracting, verified developer benchmarking, and structured pod calibration, the company has positioned itself as an indispensable pipeline for fast-growing startups in Cairo, Riyadh, Dubai, and beyond.
